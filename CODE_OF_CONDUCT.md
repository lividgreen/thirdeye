# Code of conduct

thirdeye follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and patient with other people, criticize ideas rather
than the people who hold them, and do not harass anyone.

This applies to the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and any other
place where people take part in the project.

## Reporting

To report a problem in private, write to the maintainer,
[@lividgreen](https://github.com/lividgreen), at the address on that GitHub
profile. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer decides on the response,
which can range from a private warning to a ban from the project, as the
Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
